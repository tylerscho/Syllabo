Getting started

How do I set up my subjects?

On first launch, or later from Settings, pick your six subjects and set HL or SL for each. Inside a subject, Create Unit lets you group syllabus points the way your school teaches them, and Exam countdown records upcoming tests with the topics they cover.

How do I practise one concept?

Learn › open the subject › type in the search field at the top. Each result offers Lesson, Questions, MCQ and Cards for that exact syllabus point.

How do I save a question?

Tap the bookmark on any question card while practising or in the past-paper view. Choose Unsorted or a folder, or create one on the spot. Your library is under Learn › subject › Saved questions, where you can sort by marks, type or topic and practise a whole folder.

Tutor and marking

The tutor says "Offline tutor"

Add a Claude or Gemini API key in Settings › AI tutor. Without one the tutor still explains, quizzes and sets questions from the built-in curriculum, and written answers are marked offline against the mark scheme.

Where are real past papers?

The app ships original IB-format questions. Real papers are IBO copyright and are not bundled. If your school gives you access, the import tool in the project (Tools/import_pastpapers.py) converts a paper plus mark scheme into the app; you can also paste questions in under Learn › subject › Saved questions › Import.

Review lock

Nothing is locked during review

Settings › Review lock must be on, Screen Time access granted, and at least one app chosen. It only works on a real iPhone or iPad, not in the Simulator or on Mac, and only while a session, deck or exam question is open.

My apps are still locked

Open Syllabo: any lock that outlived its session is cleared as soon as the app comes to the front. Settings › Review lock › Unlock now also clears it.

Accounts and friends

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot your password? Online accounts get a 6-digit code by email; enter it with your new password. Accounts kept only on this phone are reset with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

Friends cannot find me

Check Friends › Discoverable by username is on, and share your exact @username. With online accounts, friends can be on any phone; both of you need an online account.

How do I delete my account?

Account › Delete account. This removes the account and all of its study data from the device immediately and cannot be undone.

Mac and iPad

Does it run on Mac?

Yes, as a Mac app. The Review lock is not available on Mac; everything else, including the essay workshop, is.
