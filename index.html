Built around the syllabus, not around trivia.

Every question, flashcard and lesson is tagged to an official syllabus point, so the app always knows what you have covered, what you keep getting wrong and what your next test is on.

Practice

Pick the minutes. The session picks itself.

Slide from 5 to 60 minutes. Syllabo sequences a warm-up, a lesson card, recall, application and an exam-format question, weighted towards what is weak and what is tested soon. Flashcards, MCQ, written questions, concept review, exam practice, or teach your buddy.

Learn

Search a concept, do questions on it.

Inside any subject, type "enthalpy" or "3.1" and go straight to the lesson, questions, MCQ or cards for that exact point.

Marking

Exam-format questions, marked against a mark scheme.

Paper 1, Paper 2 and essay-plan questions with command terms and mark allocations. Write your answer and AI marking credits the right idea in your own words, point by point. Then ask the tutor what you missed. Bookmark any question into folders you name.

Tutor

A tutor that already knows where you are.

Explain this, quiz me, mark this. Powered by Claude AI and free to use: it sees your subject, syllabus point, mastery and mistakes, and keeps your chat while you work through a question.

Buddy

A streak with a face.

Your buddy reacts to every answer, cheers a streak and sulks when you skip a day. Dress it in any of 107 skins, each standing in its own illustrated scene, and teach it a topic to prove you understand it.

Revision you actually want to open.

Every question you answer earns XP and coins. They feed a weekly league, duels with friends, daily rings and a buddy you dress up. Nothing here can be bought with real money: the only way to win is to study.

Leagues

A new race every week.

You are placed in a bracket of up to 30 students at your level. XP from every session counts, the top quarter climb a badge and the bottom few slip back. Sixteen badges run from Bronze up to Luminary. Or make your own league for your class or friends, share a six-letter code, and play for a weekly coin prize or a buy-in pot.

Duels

Same ten questions. Best score wins.

Challenge a friend on any topic, pick multiple choice, short or long answers, put coins in the pot and switch hints off if you want a straight test. You both have 48 hours, and the winner takes the pot.

Daily rings

Five minutes a subject.

One ring per subject, closed by minutes studied or points practised. Close them all for coins and a crate, keep it up for a perfect week, and hold the flame to start today's session and keep your streak alive.

Crates and skins

107 skins to collect.

Coins open crates, crates drop skins for your buddy, from common to legendary, each with its own scene and eight poses. Rare colourways are numbered, so your Gold #7 of 10 is the only one. A daily drop, daily login rewards and medals for the milestones.

How it works

Set up once. From then on the app decides what to show you, and you decide how long.

01

Choose your subjects and levels

Pick your six from the full UWCSEA East list. Add your school's units and your upcoming tests with the topics they cover.

02

Open the app whenever you have a gap

In a taxi, before dinner, between lessons. Slide to the time you have and start. Or search a concept and drill just that.

03

Let mastery and mistakes steer

Every answer updates a spaced-repetition score per syllabus point. Mistakes stay open until you resolve them. Exams pull their topics forward.

Questions

Are these real IB past papers?

The app ships original questions written in IB paper format with mark schemes. Real papers are IBO copyright, so they are not bundled. If your school gives you access to past papers, the included import tool converts a paper plus mark scheme into the app in one command, and they appear with their session, paper and question reference.

Is the AI tutor really free?

Yes. The AI tutor and AI marking are included at no cost, with a daily allowance to keep things fair for everyone. They need an online account, and you can turn AI off at any time in Settings.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Mostly. Practice, flashcards, lessons, your progress and on-device marking all work offline. The AI tutor and AI marking need a connection, and marking falls back to the on-device version when you are offline.

Where is my data stored?

Your study data lives on your device. If you create an online account, a backup is kept with our database provider, Supabase, so you can sign in on another device and add friends. Messages to the AI tutor and answers sent for AI marking go to Anthropic's Claude through our server, which does not keep them. The privacy policy has the details.

Which subjects are covered in depth?

Every UWCSEA East subject is selectable with its official topic outline. Biology, Chemistry, Maths AI, Economics, English A Language and Literature and Chinese B have detailed syllabus points, questions and glossaries; more are being added.

Is it affiliated with the IB?

No. "International Baccalaureate" and "IB" are trademarks of the International Baccalaureate Organization, which does not endorse this app.
